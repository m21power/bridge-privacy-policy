    Summary

    BRIDGE is a VS Code extension and a Chrome (Manifest V3) companion extension. Together they let you use Claude, ChatGPT and Google Gemini from VS Code, using accounts you are already signed in to in Chrome. No API keys are needed.

    No BRIDGE backendBRIDGE does not run its own server to process your conversations.
    Local connectionVS Code and Chrome talk on your computer at 127.0.0.1:43127.
    Providers process AIYour content goes to the provider you choose, under its own policies.

    How BRIDGE works

    You write a prompt in VS Code. The VS Code extension sends it over a local WebSocket to the Chrome extension. The Chrome extension then uses your open, signed-in tab for the chosen provider (Claude.ai, ChatGPT or Gemini) and passes the reply back to VS Code the same way.

    Because it works through your browser session, the provider sees the activity as coming from your account in your browser. BRIDGE does not ask for, and does not need, an API key.

    Sessions and sign-in

    BRIDGE relies on sessions you have already created by signing in to each provider in Chrome. Sign-in happens on the provider's own website, not in BRIDGE.

    • Passwords. BRIDGE does not ask for or collect your provider passwords.
    • Cookies and tokens. BRIDGE does not read, copy, store or transmit your session cookies or tokens. Your session stays in Chrome.
    • Your plan. Usage counts against your own account, and the provider's usage rules apply to you. Please check that using a provider through a browser extension fits its terms.

    Local communication

    The two extensions connect through a WebSocket on the loopback address 127.0.0.1, port 43127. Loopback traffic stays on your computer and is not sent over the internet by BRIDGE.

    • A pairing token links your VS Code and Chrome extensions, so the connection is not open to anything that happens to reach the port.

    Chrome permissions

    The Chrome extension asks for the following, and uses each one only for BRIDGE's own features.

    • Site access. Claude.ai, ChatGPT (chatgpt.com and chat.openai.com), Gemini, and 127.0.0.1:43127 for the local connection. Nothing else.
    • tabs. To find your open provider tabs and read their web addresses, so BRIDGE knows which provider a tab is and whether you are signed in.
    • scripting. To run BRIDGE's scripts on those provider pages.
    • storage. To keep the extension's own settings, and to hold copied text temporarily for the save prompt.
    • alarms. To keep the connection to VS Code alive in the background.

    On the provider pages, BRIDGE's scripts send your prompts, read replies, detect when you copy text, and show an on-page overlay and save prompt. To catch copy buttons on those sites, BRIDGE also wraps the page's clipboard-write function so it can see what was copied. This applies only to the supported provider pages.

    Storage and retention

    BRIDGE

    BRIDGE does not run a server and does not keep copies of your conversations on infrastructure it operates. Inside VS Code, BRIDGE holds your conversation only temporarily on your computer. It is cleared when you close VS Code and does not come back when you reopen it.

    AI providers

    Even after BRIDGE clears its temporary data, your conversations may still exist with the provider, which stores and retains them under its policy and your settings. Deleting something in BRIDGE, if supported, may not delete it from the provider.

    Security

    • Local traffic uses the loopback interface only.
    • Connections to providers use their websites' HTTPS, handled by your browser.
    • The Chrome extension uses Manifest V3 and is limited to the sites listed above.
    • A pairing token links VS Code and Chrome.

    No software is perfectly secure. BRIDGE cannot guarantee protection against malware on your device or issues at a provider. Keep your system, browser and extensions up to date.

    Your control

    • Choose what you send. Only the prompts and files you choose to include are relayed.
    • Choose the provider. Pick or switch providers for each task.
    • Disconnect. Disable or uninstall either extension to stop BRIDGE immediately.
    • Sign out. Signing out of a provider in Chrome ends BRIDGE's access to that account.
    • Manage history. Review and delete conversations in each provider's account settings.
    • Local data. BRIDGE's temporary data in VS Code clears when you close it. You can clear the Chrome extension's data in Chrome's extension settings.

    Third parties

    BRIDGE works with Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT) and Google (Gemini). Their handling of your data is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, linked above. Chrome and VS Code are separate products with their own policies. BRIDGE does not contact any other service.

    Sale and advertising

    BRIDGE does not sell your personal data and does not use it for advertising.

    Children

    BRIDGE is built for developers and is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age in your region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The AI providers have their own age requirements, which apply when you use their services.
